HYDROGEN-POWERED JCB HYDROMAX SETS 406.320 MPH WORLD LAND SPEED RECORD - JCB
JCB has set a new world land speed record with its hydrogen-powered JCB Hydromax, reaching an average speed of 406.320 mph (653.5 km/h) at the Bonneville Salt Flats. Powered by two production-based JCB hydrogen engines delivering a combined 1,600 bhp, the achievement demonstrates the potential of hydrogen combustion technology for high-performance, zero-CO₂ tailpipe-emission applications.
